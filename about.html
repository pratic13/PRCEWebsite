About us

An engineering consultancy based in Kharadi, Pune, working for government departments, municipal bodies and private owners across Maharashtra.

Who we are

P R Consulting Engineers LLP (PRCE) takes on survey, investigation, design and project management work for public infrastructure. Most of our jobs are rural roads under PMGSY and MMGSY, irrigation surveys for the Water Resources Department, and consultancy for municipal councils.

We work alongside P R Associates (PRA), our associate firm led by Ramesh Gangarde, which holds a share of the road and irrigation contracts, and with their joint venture with CDF Infracon on PMU work. The projects page shows which firm holds each contract.

PRCE is led by Pratik Gangarde, Managing Partner. Our team includes surveyors, highway, hydraulic and bridge engineers, and CAD and GIS staff, working from the Kharadi office and on site.

How we work

We do our own fieldwork, so the engineer who designs a road has seen it. Designs follow IRC, MoRTH and the department’s own standards, and estimates use the current schedule of rates. Drawings are produced in Civil 3D and InfraWorks, and maps in QGIS.
