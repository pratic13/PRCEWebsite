About us

A survey, testing and consulting engineering firm in Kharadi, Pune.

Who we are

Engineers who do their own fieldwork

P R Consulting Engineers LLP (PRCE) surveys, tests, designs and supervises infrastructure: rural roads and highways, bridges, canals and lift irrigation, village water supply, municipal works and industrial sites.

We work alongside our associate firm P R Associates (PRA) and their joint venture with CDF Infracon. Together the group has taken on 104 assignments since 2019 and completed 73 of them.

PRCE is led by Pratik Gangarde, Managing Partner. Our team includes surveyors, highway, hydraulic, bridge and geotechnical engineers, and CAD and GIS staff, working from our Kharadi office and on site.

How we work

Standards, software and our own equipment

Designs follow IRC, MoRTH and departmental standards, and estimates use the current schedule of rates. Drawings are produced in Civil 3D and InfraWorks, water networks in WaterGEMS and maps in QGIS. Our team has also run Civil 3D training for MIT Art, Design and Technology University.

  • Total stations, DGPS receivers and survey drones
  • Network survey vehicle, FWD, Benkelman beam and bump integrator
  • Drilling and testing partners for geotechnical work
